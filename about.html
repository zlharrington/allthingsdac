About us

Family owned. Trade built.

All Things Drywall & Construction is a family-operated Tri-Cities contractor built on decades of hands-on experience in drywall, steel stud framing and general construction.

Since 1991

Experience earned on real jobsites.

Owner Matthew Peters has worked throughout the Tri-Cities and Mid-Columbia on projects ranging from schools and colleges to Hanford-area work, wineries, retail tenant improvements, industrial facilities, courthouses, power facilities, hotels and residential remodels.

After more than 25 years working in the trades for others, Matt brought that experience together with the talents of his sons to build a family business focused on strong relationships, professional work and dependable execution.

The company’s specialty is steel stud framing and drywall, with additional scopes available when they make sense for the project.
