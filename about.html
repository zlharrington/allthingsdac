About us

A family construction company.

All Things Drywall & Construction is a family-owned Tri-Cities contractor built on more than three decades of hands-on construction experience.

Owner

Matthew Peters

Matthew has more than 30 years of experience in drywall and construction. He remains directly involved in estimating, project coordination and field operations, helping ensure each project receives experienced oversight from start to finish.

His background includes commercial tenant improvements, public-sector work, schools and colleges, industrial facilities, wineries, hotels and select residential projects throughout the Tri-Cities and Mid-Columbia.

Today, the company combines Matthew’s experience with the next generation of the Peters family, with steel stud framing and drywall remaining at the center of the business.
